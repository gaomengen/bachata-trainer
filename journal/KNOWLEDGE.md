## Leading & following
- **Lead from the body, not the arms.** Moving the frame and chest gives a clear signal;
  pushing with the arms feels like a shove. *(2026-10-07)*
- **Signal early.** The follow needs to know what's coming on count 1, not count 5.
  A change of direction in the leader's first step is a clear, early signal.
  *(2026-10-07)*

## Steps & patterns
### Box step
- **Diagonal entry:** step diagonally forward-left on 1, then finish the regular box.
  The follow reads it straight away. *(2026-10-07)*
- **All diagonal:** the whole box turned 45°, including the diagonal back.
  *(2026-10-07)*
- The diagonal makes the box smaller, which helps on a crowded floor. *(2026-10-07)*
