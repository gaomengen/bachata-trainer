# My Bachata Vocabulary

Every move I know, by name, with a one-line cue. The goal is to read a name and do the
move without the video (see the 2026-10-14 journal entry: *you don't talk with a
dictionary*).

**How to practise:** read a name, do the move from memory, then update its status.
If I can't do it without the recording, it goes back to drilling.

**Status levels**
- 🎥 **With video:** I can do it while watching the recording
- 🧠 **From memory:** I can do it from the name alone, solo, even with my attention
  on the music (the "absorbed" test)
- 💃 **Social-ready:** I've led it at a social, on beat, with a stranger

Moves are grouped by style: **sensual** (partner patterns, what the LA socials dance)
and **Dominican / traditional** (footwork and expression).
