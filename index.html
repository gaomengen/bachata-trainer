The Three Rhythms

Bachata songs move through these sections. Musicality = matching your energy to whichever one is playing right now.

Verses · foundation

Derecho

Sound: Calm and grounded. The güira scrapes an even "shhh-shhh-shhh" on every beat, the bongó rolls gently, bass on 1 & 3. Predictable and smooth.

Dance it: Clean basics, close connection, subtle body rolls. Home base.

Choruses · building

Majao (mah-HOW)

Sound: The song opens up. Bongó hits the main beats harder with accents on 2 & 4, feeling syncopated and driving you forward.

Dance it: Open position, turns, bigger movements, personality and play.

Instrumental break · showtime

Mambo

Sound: High energy. The requinto guitar takes off, güira plays rapid-fire, bongó goes double-time — almost merengue-fast and intense.

Dance it: Shines, footwork, quick syncopations, fast turns. Show off.

These synthesized grooves are for practice. For real songs, try artists like Aventura, Romeo Santos, Prince Royce, or traditional Dominican bachata and listen for the same three sections.
