I'm learning bachata and keeping a journal of everything I pick up in classes, at socials and from videos. These are the lessons from it that I think will help other dancers too. I'm a learner, not a teacher, so take them as notes from someone a few steps ahead of you, and check anything important with an instructor you trust.
